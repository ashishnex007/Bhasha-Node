Organic farming enhances biological soil activity and plant immunity without synthetic inputs. Jeevamrut (जीवामृत) is an exceptional microbial bio-stimulant. To prepare 200 liters of Jeevamrut: in a 200-liter plastic drum, mix 10 kg of fresh desi cow dung, 10 liters of desi cow urine, 2 kg of organic jaggery, 2 kg of pulse flour (besan/gram flour), and a handful (100 grams) of virgin fertile soil from a bund or banyan tree root zone. Fill the drum with 200 liters of water and stir clockwise twice daily for 10 minutes. Keep in shade covered with a jute gunny bag for 48 to 72 hours for fermentation. Apply 200 liters per acre with irrigation water (or drip system after 3-stage filtration) twice a month. For seed treatment (बीजप्रक्रिया), treat 1 kg of seeds with 5 grams of Trichoderma viride or Pseudomonas fluorescens to prevent seed-borne and soil-borne fungal diseases such as root rot, collar rot, and damping-off. For legumes like soybean and chickpea, add 10 grams of Rhizobium culture per kg of seed to maximize biological nitrogen fixation in root nodules.
