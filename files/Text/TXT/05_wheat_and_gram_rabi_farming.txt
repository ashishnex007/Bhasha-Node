Rabi season crops such as Gram (Chickpea / हरभरा) and Wheat (गहू) provide critical income during the winter cycle. For Chickpea (Gram), the optimum sowing window is between October 15 and November 10. Recommended wilt-resistant varieties include Digvijay, Vijay, and Jaki 9218. Sowing depth should be 6 to 8 cm to utilize residual soil moisture. Provide one protective irrigation at 30 to 35 days (pre-flowering stage) and a second irrigation at 55 to 60 days (pod filling stage). Avoid irrigation during active flowering to prevent flower drop. For Wheat, the ideal sowing time is November 1 to November 20. Seed rate is 100 to 125 kg per hectare with a row-to-row spacing of 20 to 22.5 cm. Critical irrigation stages for wheat are: 1st at Crown Root Initiation (CRI stage at 20 to 22 days after sowing - most critical), 2nd at Tillering stage (40 to 45 days), 3rd at Late Jointing stage (60 to 65 days), 4th at Flowering stage (75 to 80 days), and 5th at Milking/Grain filling stage (90 to 95 days).
