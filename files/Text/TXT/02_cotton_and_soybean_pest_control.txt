Integrated pest management in cotton and soybean crops prevents severe yield loss while reducing chemical spray costs. In cotton, the pink bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) attacks flower buds and developing bolls. Install 5 to 8 pheromone traps per acre at 45 days after sowing for pest monitoring. If trap catches exceed 8 moths per trap per day for three consecutive days or 10 percent infested green bolls are observed, spray 5 percent Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) or neem oil (1500 ppm) at 5 ml per liter of water. For heavy bollworm infestation, apply Emamectin Benzoate 5 percent SG at 4 grams per 10 liters of water. In soybean, the major pests are stem fly, girdle beetle, and Spodoptera litura caterpillar. Install yellow sticky traps (10 to 12 per acre) to trap whiteflies and aphids that transmit the Yellow Mosaic Virus. For girdle beetle and semilooper caterpillars, spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 percent SC at 3 ml per 10 liters of water or apply Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) formulation at 2 grams per liter during early larval stages.
