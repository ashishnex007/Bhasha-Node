Proper dairy cattle management requires balanced feeding and strict healthcare practices. For a milking cow producing 10 liters of milk daily, provide 20 to 25 kg of green fodder (such as hybrid Napier or maize), 5 to 6 kg of dry fodder (jowar kadbi or wheat straw), and 4 kg of balanced concentrate feed mixed with 50 grams of mineral mixture and 30 grams of common salt. Always maintain a green-to-dry fodder ratio of 2 to 1 for optimal rumen digestion and fat percentage. Clean drinking water must be available at all times, with cows consuming 70 to 80 liters per day. To prevent mastitis (udder infection), wash the teats with clean water before milking, dry them with a clean cloth, and practice post-milking teat dipping using a 0.5 percent povidone-iodine solution. Deworm the animals every 6 months before monsoon and winter seasons using Albendazole or Fenbendazole. Vaccinate cattle against Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD) twice a year in February and August, and vaccinate against Hemorrhagic Septicemia (HS) and Black Quarter (BQ) once a year before the onset of monsoon in May.
